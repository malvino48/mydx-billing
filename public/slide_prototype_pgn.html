🔍 Problem Statement

11 Pain Points Proses Billing Gas Bumi

Setiap pain point teridentifikasi dari blueprint discovery dan telah dipetakan ke solusi teknis yang dapat diuji langsung di prototype

P01
Repeated Recalculation
Kalkulasi berulang tanpa version control
P02
Tariff Period Uncertainty
Ketidakpastian tarif saat pergantian regulasi
P03
Multiple Price & Formula
Banyak kontrak dengan formula berbeda
P04
Regulatory Change
Perubahan regulasi Permen/Kepmen ESDM
P05
Fragmented Source Data
Data tersebar & incomplete
P06
Payment Scheme Complexity
Postpaid, prepaid, hybrid
P07
UOM, Allocation & Component
Konversi m³→MMBTU, eligible/excess split
P08
Manual Validation
Approval tanpa threshold otomatis
P09
Explainability & Audit Trail
Tidak bisa trace asal perhitungan
P10
Billing Frequency
Recurring, annual (prorata), one-time
P11
Invoice Inconsistency
Invoice tanpa breakdown lengkap
💡
Semua 11 pain points dipetakan langsung ke endpoint API dan UI yang fungsional. Setiap skenario bisa dijalankan end-to-end dan hasilnya tersimpan permanen di database.
